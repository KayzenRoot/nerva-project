# ADR-0002 — Guardian Thesis vs NERVA Product Boundary

Status: APPROVED_CANDIDATE — NERVA-WO-001

## Context
The initial research produced a broad Guardian concept spanning automation, risk, yield, insurance, payments and multi-protocol autonomous finance. The hackathon and company need a focused product that can ship.

## Decision
Guardian is retained as the long-horizon architectural thesis. NERVA is the product/company surface and V0.1 implementation. V0.1 focuses on Monad + Perpl position risk, policy-driven protective action, agent-wallet execution and verifiable evidence.

Future Guardian capabilities enter NERVA only through later scoped versions.

## Consequences
The roadmap remains ambitious without placing every idea into the hackathon build. Product naming and code/docs use NERVA; "Guardian" may appear as thesis/history, not as a second competing application.
